## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What's the difference between this guide and the old manual one?**
> **A:** First, this automated `.zip` method is significantly safer and faster than typing manual terminal commands. Second, this specific partition layout gives us much more space to accommodate upcoming, heavier Android ports.

**Q: If the last guide was for 7GB and this one is the same, what is the difference? Should I redo it?**
> **A:** Yes, you should redo it. While the system size might look similar, this new script allocates more space to the **Vendor** partition. As modern ports get bigger and bigger, they require a larger Vendor partition. We heavily advise everyone to switch to this new layout sooner rather than later, as all future `Android Ports` releases will depend on it.

**Q: Which guide is for my specific device variant (Global, Regional, etc.)?**
> **A:** This guide is **universal**. All variants of the POCO F1 and Xiaomi Mi 8 are eligible and supported by this automated script.
