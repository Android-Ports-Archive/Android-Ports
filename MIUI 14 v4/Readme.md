## 🛠️ Flashing Instructions

> 🛑 **IMPORTANT:** A clean flash is strictly mandatory for MIUI ports to avoid bootloops and system glitches.

1. Boot into your custom recovery (OrangeFox / TWRP).
2. Go to **Wipe** > **Advanced Wipe**.
3. Select and wipe: `Dalvik / ART Cache`, `Cache`, `System`, `Vendor`, and `Data`.
4. Flash the MIUI 14 `.zip` file.
5. **Format Data** (Type `yes`).
6. Reboot to System.
