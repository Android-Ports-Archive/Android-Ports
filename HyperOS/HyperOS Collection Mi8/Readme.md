## 🛠️ Flashing Instructions

> ⚠️ **REMINDER:** These builds are provided completely as-is with no developer support.

1. Boot into your custom recovery (TWRP/OrangeFox).
2. Wipe necessary partitions (Dalvik, Cache, System, Data).
3. **Flash the ROM `.zip` file** of your choice.
4. **Format Data** (Type `yes`).
5. Reboot to System.
